# Browsing
Source: https://developer.android.com/design/ui/gallery/reading/browsing

Enable book buyers to scan row after row of the latest editions in a large multi-column grid that showcases best sellers or best bargains with prominent size and position. Categorize books in rows or columns for easy identification. Include side navigation to put stacks of paperbacks at shoppers' tapping or mouse-clicking fingertips.

Make book browsing informative and engaging by including synopses or text excerpts for select titles in multi-title book listings.
