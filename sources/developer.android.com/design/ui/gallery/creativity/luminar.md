# Interactive photo editing experience
Source: https://developer.android.com/design/ui/gallery/creativity/luminar

[IMAGE] Custom photo editing controls

Designed with Android's flexibility in mind, Luminar delivers a seamless editing experience across phones, tablets, and large screens. The interface moves beyond standard controls, introducing photo-centric sliders, tactile gestures, and immersive feedback that keep the image at the center of every interaction. Whether you're refining details or exploring creative directions, you stay in a continuous, fluid editing flow.

[IMAGE] Controls and navigation layout adapt

Playful, intuitive interactions and film-inspired visual language deepen engagement, making presets feel tangible and familiar. Subtle sound design reinforces each action, providing responsive feedback without disrupting focus.

[IMAGE] Luminar across device sizes

Together, these elements create an experience that balances precision with experimentation, guiding you from the first adjustment to the final image.
