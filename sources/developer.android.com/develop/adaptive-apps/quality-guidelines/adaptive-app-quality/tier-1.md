# Tier 1 — Adaptive differentiated
Source: https://developer.android.com/develop/adaptive-apps/quality-guidelines/adaptive-app-quality/tier-1

Adaptive differentiated apps use the large screen and foldable form factors to their full potential. Differentiated apps offer a premium user experience that's productive and delightful.

[IMAGE] Depiction of the three quality tiers as layers stacked vertically with the top tier highlighted.

Because Tier 1 apps are highly differentiated, the Tier 1 guidelines are organized by app _experiences_—the type of app you're creating or the type of device you're targeting.

Choose the experiences that are right for your application:

-       Desktop
-       Foldables
-       Camera • Audio

-       Stylus
-       Cars
-       TV

-       XR
-       Games
-       Widgets
