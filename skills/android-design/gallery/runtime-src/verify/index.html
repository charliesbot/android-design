m3e-button

Compose: Button, ElevatedButton, FilledTonalButton, OutlinedButton, TextButton, and the toggle button family.

m3e-button-group

Compose: ButtonGroup for standard, a Row of toggle buttons with connected shapes for connected.

m3e-icon-button

Compose: IconButton, FilledIconButton, FilledTonalIconButton, OutlinedIconButton, and the IconToggleButton family. Elevated has no Compose counterpart.

m3e-split-button

Compose: SplitButtonLayout with SplitButtonDefaults leading and trailing buttons. The trailing glyph is a stand-in: the icon subset has no chevron.

m3e-fab

Compose: FloatingActionButton, SmallFloatingActionButton, LargeFloatingActionButton, ExtendedFloatingActionButton.

m3e-fab-menu, m3e-fab-menu-item, m3e-fab-menu-trigger

Compose: FloatingActionButtonMenu with ToggleFloatingActionButton and FloatingActionButtonMenuItem. Click the FAB to open.

m3e-card

Compose: Card, ElevatedCard, OutlinedCard. Actionable cards take onClick.

m3e-list, m3e-list-item, m3e-action-list, m3e-list-action, m3e-selection-list, m3e-list-option, m3e-expandable-list-item

Compose: ListItem, SegmentedListItem. The expandable item has no Compose counterpart.

m3e-chip, m3e-chip-set, m3e-assist-chip, m3e-filter-chip, m3e-filter-chip-set, m3e-input-chip, m3e-input-chip-set, m3e-suggestion-chip

Compose: AssistChip, FilterChip, InputChip, SuggestionChip and their Elevated forms. The non-interactive chip has no Compose counterpart.

m3e-toolbar and p-floating-toolbar

Compose: HorizontalFloatingToolbar and VerticalFloatingToolbar. The floating wrapper is shown in place here; on a proposal page it sits at the bottom center.

m3e-nav-rail, m3e-nav-item, m3e-nav-rail-toggle

Compose: WideNavigationRail with WideNavigationRailItem. Compact is railExpanded = false.

m3e-nav-bar

Compose: ShortNavigationBar with ShortNavigationBarItem.

m3e-app-bar

Compose: TopAppBar, CenterAlignedTopAppBar, MediumFlexibleTopAppBar, LargeFlexibleTopAppBar.

m3e-loading-indicator

Compose: LoadingIndicator, ContainedLoadingIndicator.

m3e-circular-progress-indicator and m3e-linear-progress-indicator

Compose: CircularProgressIndicator, CircularWavyProgressIndicator, LinearProgressIndicator, LinearWavyProgressIndicator.

m3e-switch

Compose: Switch.

m3e-slider and m3e-slider-thumb

Compose: Slider and RangeSlider.

m3e-shape

Compose: MaterialShapes.<Name>.toShape(). Hexagon has no counterpart.

p-icon

Compose: the same Material Symbol as a vector drawable, `Icon(painterResource(R.drawable.<name>), ...)`. Each glyph is shown unfilled and filled.

Type roles

Compose: MaterialTheme.typography.<role> and <role>Emphasized.

Color roles

Compose: MaterialTheme.colorScheme.<role>. One swatch per role the theme emits.

Corner scale

Compose: MaterialTheme.shapes.<size>; full is CircleShape and none is RectangleShape.
